My Family Cookbook

Your recipes, meal plans, and grocery lists — all in one place

My Family Cookbook is an easy-to-use, full-featured recipe organizer, meal planner, and grocery list organizer — with syncing across all of your devices and the ability to share with family and friends. There are no in-app charges; all features are included within the initial purchase price.

I built this app to honor my mother, may she rest in peace, who was a wonderful chef and cooking teacher. The app includes several default recipes intended to share some of her timeless recipes.

Sharing & Syncing

Your recipes, meal planner, and grocery list can be synced across your devices and shared with family members and friends.

Sync Across Your Devices

Syncing occurs automatically across all of your devices. When you make changes to your recipes, meal planner, or grocery list on one device, those changes are automatically reflected on your other devices — including the devices of family members and friends with whom you've shared your cookbook.

Plan on One Device, Shop on Another

For example, you can do your meal planning and build your grocery list on your iPad. Your meal plan, grocery list, and recipes will then be available on your iPhone when you — or a family member you're sharing with — head to the grocery store.

Home View

The Home View displays all of your recipes. Tap any recipe to view the details for that recipe.

Home View Features

Search

Use the search bar to filter your recipes by recipe name.

Filter by Category

Use the filter icon to filter your recipes by recipe category.

Favorites

Use the favorite icon to show only recipes you've marked as favorites.

Tip: You can use the category filter and favorites filter together. For example, select a recipe category and then select Favorites to show only your favorite recipes within that category.

Recipe Detail View

The Recipe Detail View displays all of the information for the selected recipe. Use the back arrow in the upper-left corner to return to the Home View.

Recipe Detail View Features

Favorite

Mark or unmark the recipe as a favorite.

Edit Recipe

Edit the recipe's details, including its ingredients, instructions, and other information.

Delete Recipe

Delete the recipe from your cookbook.

Add Recipe Ingredients: The Recipe Detail View also includes an Add Recipe Ingredients feature that lets you select ingredients from the recipe and add them directly to your grocery list.

Grocery List View

The Grocery List View displays your current grocery list, grouped by your grocery items and by recipe.

You can add your own grocery items directly from this view, and swipe left on an individual item to delete it. Ingredients can also be added to the grocery list from an individual recipe or from recipes in next week's meal plan.

Grocery List View Features

Filter

Limit the grocery list to items from a specific recipe or to your own grocery items.

Add Meal Planner Recipes

Add ingredients from the recipes in next week's meal plan to your grocery list.

Normalize & Combine Ingredients

Normalize ingredient names and combine matching ingredients to simplify your grocery list.

Erase Selected Ingredients

Remove selected ingredients from the grocery list.

Clear Grocery List

Delete all items from the current grocery list.

Categorize Ingredient

Assign a grocery category to an ingredient to keep your shopping list organized.

Tip: If you change the category of an ingredient, My Family Cookbook remembers that choice and uses the same category the next time that ingredient is added to the grocery list.

iCloud & Devices

My Family Cookbook can keep your cookbook available across your supported Apple devices using iCloud.

Make sure you are signed into the appropriate iCloud account on your devices and that iCloud is enabled.

Sharing

My Family Cookbook allows you to share your cookbook with family and friends so everyone can have access to shared recipes and grocery information.
