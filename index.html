Getting Started

My Family Cookbook gives you one place to organize your recipes, plan meals, create grocery lists, and keep your cookbook available across your devices.

Adding Your First Recipe

You can add recipes manually or import recipes from supported websites. Recipes can include ingredients, instructions, notes, categories, and photos.

Recipes

Your cookbook keeps all of your recipes organized and easy to find.

Adding a Recipe

Create your own recipe by entering the recipe name, ingredients, instructions, category, and other information.

Importing a Recipe

Recipes can also be imported from supported recipe websites, saving you from entering the recipe manually.

Editing a Recipe

Open a recipe and choose Edit to update its ingredients, instructions, notes, category, or other information.

Meal Planning

The Meal Planner helps you organize meals for the week using recipes already stored in your cookbook.

Add recipes to individual days to create a simple weekly meal plan and make it easier to decide what to cook.

Grocery Lists

Add ingredients from your recipes directly to your grocery list so you don't have to enter each ingredient individually.

You can also add your own grocery items for things that aren't part of a recipe.

iCloud & Devices

My Family Cookbook can keep your cookbook available across your supported Apple devices using iCloud.

Make sure you are signed into the appropriate iCloud account on your devices and that iCloud is enabled.

Sharing

My Family Cookbook allows you to share your cookbook with family and friends so everyone can have access to shared recipes and grocery information.
