Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about My Family's Cookbook.

Getting Started

What is My Family's Cookbook?

My Family's Cookbook is a recipe organizer, meal planner, and grocery list app designed to keep your recipes and meal planning in one place.

You can create your own recipes, import recipes from websites, plan meals, create grocery lists, and use your cookbook across your supported Apple devices.

Does the app include any recipes?

Yes. My Family's Cookbook includes a collection of default recipes that you can add to your cookbook.

You can also create your own recipes and import recipes from supported websites.

Do I need to create an account?

No. My Family's Cookbook does not require you to create a separate account.

Apple's iCloud services are used for syncing your cookbook between your devices and for cookbook sharing.

Recipes

How do I add my own recipe?

Choose New Recipe from the menu. Enter your recipe information, including the ingredients and instructions, and save it to your cookbook.

Can I import a recipe from a website?

Yes. Choose Import from Web and enter the web address of the recipe.

My Family's Cookbook will attempt to retrieve the recipe information and let you review it before saving it to your cookbook.

Why didn't a recipe import correctly?

Recipe websites use many different formats. My Family's Cookbook recognizes industry standard recipe formats, but some websites may prevent access or structure their recipe information in non-standard ways.

You can review and edit an imported recipe before saving it to your cookbook.

Can I edit or delete a recipe?

Yes. Open a recipe to view its details. From there, you can edit the recipe or delete it from your cookbook.

How do Favorites work?

You can mark a recipe as a favorite from the Recipe Detail view.

The Home view can then be filtered to show only your favorite recipes.

What Can I Make?

What is What Can I Make?

What Can I Make? helps you find recipes based on ingredients you already have.

Enter the major ingredients you have available and the app will search your cookbook for recipes you may be able to make.

Do I need to enter every ingredient I have?

No. Concentrate on the main ingredients you want to use. You do not need to enter everything in your pantry or refrigerator.

Why isn't a recipe appearing in What Can I Make?

What Can I Make? compares the ingredients you enter with the ingredients in your saved recipes.

Differences in ingredient names or unusually formatted ingredients can sometimes prevent a match. Try entering a simpler ingredient name when possible.

Grocery List

How do I add recipe ingredients to my grocery list?

You can add ingredients from a recipe directly to your grocery list. My Family's Cookbook processes the ingredients and combines matching items.

Can I add items to the grocery list manually?

Yes. You can add items directly to your grocery list, even when they are not part of a recipe.

Why are grocery items organized into categories?

Grocery items are automatically organized into categories such as Produce, Bakery, Dairy & Eggs, Meat & Seafood, Pantry, Frozen, Beverages, Household, and Other to make your list easier to use while shopping.

Can I change the category of a grocery item?

Yes. If an item is placed in the wrong category, you can change its category from the grocery list.

Meal Planner

How does the Meal Planner work?

The Meal Planner lets you assign recipes from your cookbook to specific days so you can plan upcoming meals.

iCloud & Syncing

Does My Family's Cookbook sync between my devices?

Yes. Your cookbook will automatically sync across your supported Apple devices using iCloud.

Make sure your devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, have iCloud enabled, and have an internet connection.

Why don't changes appear immediately on my other device?

iCloud synchronization may take a little time. Make sure both devices have an internet connection and are signed in to the appropriate iCloud account.

Opening My Family's Cookbook on both devices can also allow pending changes to synchronize.

Sharing

Can I share my cookbook with family and friends?

Yes. My Family's Cookbook allows you to share your cookbook with family members or friends using iCloud sharing.

Sharing options are available from Settings.

Does the person I share with need My Family's Cookbook?

Yes. The person you share with needs My Family's Cookbook installed on their device and access to iCloud to participate in the shared cookbook.

How do I manage or stop sharing my cookbook?

Open Settings and choose Manage Sharing.

From there, you can view and manage the people who have access to your shared cookbook.

Purchases & Support

Does My Family's Cookbook contain ads?

No. My Family's Cookbook does not contain advertising.

Are there subscriptions or in-app purchases?

No. There are no subscriptions or additional in-app charges. All features are included with the initial purchase of the app.

Where can I find detailed instructions?

Visit the Guide page for detailed instructions covering the major features of My Family's Cookbook.

How do I report a problem or ask a question?

Use the Contact Us page to report a problem, ask a question, or send feedback.

When reporting a problem, please include as much detail as possible about what you were doing when the problem occurred. Including a screenshot can also be helpful.
