Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about My Family Cookbook.

Does My Family Cookbook sync between my devices?

My Family Cookbook can use iCloud to keep your cookbook available across supported Apple devices.

Make sure the devices are signed into the appropriate iCloud account and that iCloud is enabled.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. You can create your own recipes and add ingredients, instructions, categories, notes, photos, and other recipe information.

Can I import recipes from websites?

Yes. My Family Cookbook can import recipes from supported recipe websites, which can save you from entering the recipe manually.

How do I add recipe ingredients to my grocery list?

You can add ingredients from your saved recipes directly to your grocery list.

My Family Cookbook can also combine matching ingredients to help simplify your shopping list.

Can I add grocery items that aren't part of a recipe?

Yes. You can add your own items directly to the grocery list for household items, snacks, supplies, or anything else you need to purchase.

Can I plan meals for the week?

Yes. The Meal Planner lets you organize meals by day using recipes from your cookbook.

Can I share my cookbook with family and friends?

My Family Cookbook supports sharing so you can make cookbook information available to family members or other people you choose.

Do I need an internet connection to use the app?

Many features of the app can be used without an active internet connection. Features that depend on iCloud synchronization, sharing, or importing recipes from websites require an internet connection.

Why isn't a change appearing on another device?

First, confirm that both devices have an internet connection and are signed into the appropriate iCloud account.

If the change still does not appear, open My Family Cookbook on both devices and allow time for iCloud synchronization to complete.

How do I get additional help?

If you can't find the answer in the documentation or FAQ, please contact support.
