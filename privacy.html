Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

My Family Cookbook App

My Family Cookbook does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Information you enter into the app, including recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, notes, and other cookbook information, is used solely to provide the app's functionality.

iCloud

My Family Cookbook may use Apple's iCloud services to synchronize your cookbook information across your devices and to support features such as sharing.

Your iCloud data is managed through Apple's services and your Apple Account. My Family Cookbook does not operate its own server that collects or stores your cookbook information.

Analytics and Tracking

My Family Cookbook does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies to monitor your use of the app.

Advertising

My Family Cookbook does not display third-party advertising.

Support Website

If you choose to contact My Family Cookbook Support through this website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, device information, and the contents of your support request.

This information is used only to respond to your support request. Support form submissions are processed using Formspree, a third-party form processing service.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on services provided by Apple, such as iCloud, to provide synchronization and sharing functionality. Use of those services is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

The support website uses Formspree to process messages submitted through the Contact Support form.

Children's Privacy

My Family Cookbook does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or privacy practices of My Family Cookbook change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact My Family Cookbook Support.
