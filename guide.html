User Guide

Learn how to use My Family's Cookbook and its features.

Home View

The Home View displays all of your recipes. Tap any recipe to view the details for that recipe.

Home View Features

Search

Use the search bar to find recipes by title, author, or category.

Filter by Category

Use the filter icon to filter your recipes by recipe category.

Favorites

Use the favorite icon to show only recipes you've marked as favorites.

Share Recipes

Select individual recipes to export and share with friends. Only the selected recipes are shared, not your grocery list or meal planner.

Tip: You can use the category filter and favorites filter together. For example, select a recipe category and then Favorites to show only your favorite recipes within that category.
Recipe Detail View

The Recipe Detail View displays all of the information for the selected recipe. Use the back arrow in the upper-left corner to return to the previous view.

Recipe Detail View Features

Favorite

Mark or unmark the recipe as a favorite.

Edit Recipe

Edit the recipe's details, including its ingredients, instructions, and other information.

Share Recipe

Export the current recipe to share with friends or family members. You can send an importable recipe file and a PDF copy for viewing or printing.

Delete Recipe

Delete the recipe from your cookbook.

Tips:

1) Use 'Add Ingredients to Grocery List' to select the ingredients you want to add from a recipe. All ingredients are selected by default, but you can deselect any ingredients you don't need.

2) Tap 'Add Selected' to automatically parse, categorize, and combine the selected ingredients with matching items already in your shopping list. No additional parsing step is needed.

3) In the Grocery List View, tap an item to see the original ingredient measurements, preparations, and the recipe it came from.

4) To share an individual recipe with a friend, tap 'Share Recipe' in the toolbar. Send the exported recipe file to your friend, who can import it using 'Import Shared Recipes' from the menu.

5) Sharing an individual recipe does not share your grocery list or meal planner and does not create an ongoing shared cookbook connection.

Grocery List View

The Grocery List View displays your shopping list organized by grocery category.

You can add your own grocery items directly from this view or import ingredients from individual recipes and recipes in your meal planner. Imported ingredients are automatically parsed, categorized, and combined with matching shopping list items. Swipe left on an item to delete it, or tap an item to view its original recipe ingredient details.

Using the Grocery List

The simplest way to use the grocery list is to add your own grocery items to build a shopping list. While shopping you can swipe left to delete an item after placing it in your cart, or select multiple items and use the eraser option, or use the clear option to clear your entire shopping list.

You can also add ingredients directly from a recipe using the 'Add Ingredients to Grocery List' feature in the Recipe Detail View. Select the ingredients you want to add and tap 'Add Selected'. The ingredients are automatically parsed, categorized, and combined with matching items in your shopping list. While shopping, tap an item to see the original ingredient measurements, preparations, and recipe information.

Another way to build your shopping list is to use the 'Add Meal Planner Recipes' feature in the Grocery List View. Select the recipes you want to include from your meal planner and tap 'Add Selected'. Their ingredients are automatically parsed, categorized, and combined with matching items already in your shopping list. Tap any shopping list item to see its original ingredient details and the recipes it came from.

Grocery List View Features

Filter by Category

Show all grocery items or filter your shopping list by a specific grocery category, such as Produce, Dairy & Eggs, or Pantry.

Add Meal Planner Recipes

Select recipes from next week's meal plan and automatically add their parsed and categorized ingredients to your shopping list. Matching ingredients are combined automatically.

Erase Selected Ingredients

Remove selected ingredients from the grocery list.

Clear All

Delete all items from the grocery list.

Categorize Ingredient

Assign a grocery category to an ingredient to keep your shopping list organized.

Tips:

1) Tap an ingredient added from a recipe to view its original quantity, preparation details, and the recipe it came from.

2) If you change the category of an ingredient, it will be remembered for the next time that ingredient is added to the grocery list.

3) If you add a grocery item that is not in the suggestion list, it will be remembered for the next time the suggestion list is presented.

New Recipe View

The New Recipe View is where you can enter your own recipes into. Enter the recipe details, select a category, add as many ingredients as needed, add instructions, and save the completed recipe to your cookbook.

New Recipe View Features

Recipe Category

Use the dropdown list to select the category for your recipe.

Add Ingredients

Add multiple ingredients to your recipe as needed.

Save Recipe

Save the completed recipe to My Family's Cookbook.

Meal Planner View

The Meal Planner View is where you plan your meals for the following week and view your meals for the current week.

Each day includes an add recipe feature that lets you select a meal type and choose recipes from your cookbook for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.

Meal Planner View Features

Show This Week's Plan

View the meal plan for the current week instead of next week's meal plan.

Clear Meal Planner

Delete recipes from the meal planner.

Tips:

1) Tap any recipe in the Meal Planner to quickly view the recipe details.

2) To remove just one recipe from the plan, swipe left on that recipe and delete it.

What Can I Make View

The What Can I Make View is where you can find recipes based on the ingredients you already have available.

What Can I Make View Features

Add Ingredients

Enter the main ingredients you have available and tap the + button. You can add as many ingredients as you want.

Find Recipes

Tap Find Recipes to compare the ingredients you entered with the recipes in your cookbook.

You Can Make Now

Recipes in this section match all of the major ingredients required by the recipe based on what you entered. You may still need common pantry staples or other ingredients listed in the full recipe.

Other Matches

These recipes match some, but not all, of the major ingredients you entered. Missing ingredients are displayed beneath each recipe to help you decide what else you may need.

Tips:

1) Enter the main ingredients you want to use rather than common pantry staples.

2) The percentage shown beside each recipe indicates how many of the recipe's major ingredients match the ingredients you entered.

3) Tap a recipe in the results to view the complete recipe.

4) Use the red minus button to remove an ingredient and run the search again.

Load Default Recipes View

The Load Default Recipes View allows you to load the default recipes supplied with My Family's Cookbook into your cookbook.

Load Recipes View Features

Load Recipes

Select which of the supplied default recipes you would like to add to your cookbook.

Tips:

1) This feature is also useful for restoring default recipes that you may have accidentally deleted.

2) If additional default recipes are included with future releases of My Family's Cookbook, you can use this feature after upgrading to add the new recipes while leaving all of your existing recipes in place.

Import from Web View

The Import from Web View allows you to import recipes from your favorite recipe websites and blogs without having to type them in yourself.

Importing a Recipe

Find a recipe that you want to import and copy the URL of the recipe webpage and paste it into the 'Recipe URL' field.

Tap the Fetch button and My Family's Cookbook will retrieve the available recipe information and prepare it for you to review.

Import from Web View Features

Save Recipe

Save the imported recipe to My Family's Cookbook.

Tip: After fetching a recipe, you can review and change its title, author, and recipe category as needed before saving it to your cookbook.
Import Shared Recipes View

The Import Shared Recipes View allows you to import recipes shared with you by friends or family members into your own cookbook. This does not share your grocery list or meal planner.

Importing Shared Recipes

When someone shares recipes with you, save the .familyrecipes file to a location accessible from the Files app on your device.

Open My Family's Cookbook and select 'Import Shared Recipes' from the menu. Tap 'Choose Recipe File' and select the file.

Review the recipes and choose how to handle any possible duplicates. Tap 'Import Recipes' to add the selected recipes to your cookbook.

Import Shared Recipes View Features

Choose Recipe File

Select a .familyrecipes file containing recipes shared with you.

Review Recipes

Review the recipes in the selected file before importing them.

Import Recipes

Import the recipes using the actions you've selected.

Handling Duplicate Recipes

If an imported recipe has the same title and author as an existing recipe, it is marked as a possible duplicate.

Skip: Do not import the duplicate recipe. This is the default action.

Import as New: Add another copy without changing the existing recipe.

Replace Existing: Replace the existing recipe's details with those from the imported recipe. Confirmation is required.

Tips:

1) Recipes that are not duplicates are automatically marked for import.

2) Replacing an existing recipe preserves its favorite status.

3) Imported recipes become part of your cookbook and can sync through iCloud.

4) The original shared recipe file is not deleted after importing.

Settings View

The Settings View contains options for sharing your complete cookbook with household family members, managing existing cookbook sharing, and managing learned groceries and ingredient category overrides.

Settings View Features

Share Cookbook

Share your complete cookbook with family members in your household. Recipes, the grocery list, and meal planner are shared and kept in sync through iCloud.

Manage Sharing

Manage who has access to your shared household cookbook, adjust sharing permissions, or stop sharing.

Manage Learned Groceries

View groceries the app has learned from items you've entered, and remove any you no longer want included in grocery suggestions.

Manage Category Overrides

View category choices the app has remembered when you've changed a grocery item's category, and remove any you no longer want remembered.

Sharing and Syncing

My Family's Cookbook provides two ways to share: Share Cookbook for household family members, and Share Recipes for exchanging individual recipes with friends.

Sync Across Your Devices

Recipes, the grocery list, and meal planner automatically sync between devices using the same Apple Account through iCloud.

Share Cookbook with Your Household

Use Share Cookbook in Settings to invite household family members to share your complete cookbook. Everyone participating in the shared cookbook can access the same recipes, grocery list, and meal planner. Changes are synced through iCloud.

Plan on One Device, Shop on Another

For example, plan meals and build your grocery list on an iPad. The shared grocery list is then available on an iPhone when someone in your household goes shopping.

Setup and Manage Sharing

Sharing between devices using the same Apple ID is automatic and does not require an invitation.

To share with a household family member using a different Apple Account, tap 'Share Cookbook' in Settings. Send an invitation using the Apple iCloud sharing dialog. The recipient should install My Family's Cookbook and accept the invitation, preferably before adding their own cookbook data.

To manage an existing household cookbook share, tap 'Manage Sharing' in Settings. You can manage participants and sharing permissions or stop sharing.

Sharing Recipes with Friends

To share individual recipes without sharing your grocery list or meal planner, open the Home View and tap 'Share Recipes' in the toolbar.

Select the recipes you want to share, or tap 'Select All'. Then tap 'Share Selected' to export the recipes and open the sharing options.

Send the exported recipe file to your friend. They can use 'Import Shared Recipes' from the menu to add those recipes to their own cookbook.

Sharing individual recipes does not create an ongoing shared cookbook connection.
